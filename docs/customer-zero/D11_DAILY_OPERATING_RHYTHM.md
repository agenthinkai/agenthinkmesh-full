## Overview

The AgenThink Mesh Daily Operating Rhythm defines the cadence of automated intelligence delivery from the Decision Twin to the leadership team. It ensures that every working day starts with a structured brief and every week ends with a performance review.

---

| Report | Schedule | Kuwait Time | UTC | Handler |
|--------|----------|-------------|-----|---------|
| Daily Brief | Weekdays | 07:00 | 04:00 | `agenthinkDailyRhythm.ts` |

## Daily Brief — Content Specification

The daily brief is generated by querying the platform and delivered via owner notification. It contains:

### Section 1: Decision Queue Status
- Number of open decisions
- Decisions requiring action today (confidence < 70% or open > 3 days)
- Council sessions started in the last 24 hours

### Section 2: KPI Alerts
- Any KPI in warning status (amber)
- Any KPI in critical status (red) — flagged as urgent
- KPIs that crossed a threshold in the last 24 hours

### Section 3: Yesterday's Activity
- Twin sessions opened and closed
- Decisions made with council verdicts
- Outcomes logged to the Outcome Ledger

### Section 4: Model Training Status (Jupiter Shot)
- Latest training step and loss value
- Checkpoint integrity status
- GPU utilisation (when connector is live)

### Section 5: Action Items
- Ranked list of items requiring leadership attention
- Each item links to the relevant twin session or KPI

---

## Weekly Review — Content Specification

The weekly review is a deeper analysis delivered every Monday morning:

### Section 1: Decision Outcomes This Week
- All decisions made in the past 7 days
- Council verdicts and confidence scores
- Comparison to predicted outcomes (where available)

### Section 2: KPI Trends (7-day)
- Direction arrow for each KPI (↑ improving, ↓ declining, → stable)
- Week-over-week change percentage
- Highlight of biggest positive and negative movements

### Section 3: Outcome Ledger Accuracy
- Decisions closed this week
- Predicted vs. actual outcome comparison
- Running accuracy rate (all-time)

### Section 4: Jupiter Shot Progress
- Training steps completed this week
- Benchmark improvements (if evaluation run)
- Next milestone and estimated completion

### Section 5: Next Week Priorities
- Top 3 pending decisions
- Top 3 KPI risks
- Upcoming deadlines or commitments

## Heartbeat Configuration

```typescript
// Registered in server/_core/index.ts
heartbeat.register({
  name: "agenthink-daily-rhythm",
  description: "AgenThink Mesh Daily Operating Rhythm — morning brief and weekly review",
  path: "/api/scheduled/agenthink-daily-rhythm",
  schedule: "0 4 * * 1-5",  // Weekdays at 04:00 UTC (07:00 Kuwait)
});
```

The heartbeat is visible in **Settings → Schedules** in the Management UI and can be paused, resumed, or triggered manually from there.

---

## Escalation Protocol

If the daily brief identifies a critical KPI breach or a decision with confidence below 50%, the system sends an additional high-priority notification with the subject line prefixed `[URGENT]`. This ensures critical issues are not missed in the daily brief.
