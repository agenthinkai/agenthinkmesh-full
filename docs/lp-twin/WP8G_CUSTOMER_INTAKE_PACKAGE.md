## What LP Twin v1 Is

LP Twin v1 is a capital formation preparation tool that allows investment managers to test a
real fund profile against synthetic institutional allocator segments before beginning a
fundraising campaign.

**What it does:**
- Scores your fund against 9 synthetic LP archetypes across 18 dimensions
- Identifies likely objections before you walk into the room
- Generates structured meeting briefs and objection rehearsal scenarios
- Models the impact of fund-term changes on allocator fit
- Tracks fundraising pipeline and records actual meeting outcomes
- Provides a foundation for calibrating synthetic outputs against real allocator responses

**What it does not do:**
- Predict whether a specific LP will invest
- Replace placement agents, legal counsel, or regulatory advisers
- Guarantee fundraising outcomes
- Provide investment advice

---

## What LP Twin v1 Is Not

> All LP Twin outputs are evidence-based synthetic simulations derived from anonymised
> institutional archetypes. They are not validated predictions of real allocator behaviour.
> Do not rely on these outputs as investment advice.

The synthetic LP archetypes are constructed from publicly available information about
institutional investor behaviour. They have not been validated against real allocator
responses. The validation foundation (WP7) is in place to collect that validation data
over time.

## Onboarding Steps

1. **Account creation:** Your account will be created by the AgenThinkMesh team and assigned to your organisation.
2. **Fund profile creation:** You will create your fund profile using the 8-step wizard.
3. **First simulation:** You will run your first simulation against all 9 LP segments.
4. **Results review:** The AgenThinkMesh team will walk you through the results in a 60-minute session.
5. **Scenario testing:** You will test 2–3 fund-term scenarios in the Fund-Term Laboratory.
6. **Meeting preparation:** You will generate meeting briefs for your top 3 target LP segments.
7. **Feedback:** You will provide structured feedback on the accuracy and usefulness of the outputs.

---

## Founding Customer Commitments

As a founding validation customer, you agree to:

1. Use LP Twin v1 in connection with a real fundraising campaign or preparation process.
2. Provide structured feedback on the accuracy of synthetic LP outputs after at least 3 real LP meetings.
3. Optionally contribute anonymised actual LP response data to the validation foundation.
4. Not share LP Twin outputs publicly or with third parties without written consent.
5. Not rely on LP Twin outputs as investment advice or as a substitute for professional placement services.
